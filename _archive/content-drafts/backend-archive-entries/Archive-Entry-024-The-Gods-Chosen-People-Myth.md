# Archive Entry 024: The Gods Chosen People Myth

**Source:** import/Archive.md  
**Category:** Theology / Commentary

The gods chosen people is a myth made by man. If God is creator of all none equal to, than why would God create man and call them goyim and have to sacrifice themselves for Gods chosen? Why would he create other man? No we are All Gods chosen people. God created us equally, to develop and unify together to build that Tower of Babel.

The Tower of Babel wasn't meant to oppose god—no it was even greater than the pyramids of Giza themselves. If the Tower of Babel was to be as angry in the eye of God than wouldn't the pyramids of Giza and other great works be viewed as such as well?

No, these were to Honor God and let God know we are your image and we want to show the reflection of love which is consciousness and intellectual curiosity you have bestowed upon. We wish to show you what we can do in our ways to communicate with you.

The demons and evil who have manipulated man has made much of the history revered and opposite—it is actually false. The Tower of Babel was made to communicate and get closer to God. It was our first of many representations we made for God.

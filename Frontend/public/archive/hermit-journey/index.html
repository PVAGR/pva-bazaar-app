INTRODUCTION: FROM WANDERER TO VISIONARY

This is not a business plan. This is not a spiritual treatise. This is not a manifesto.

This is the story of how all of it came to be.

The man who wrote about Vimana, Bioharmonic Suits, PVA Bazaar, and the Taur Community was not born with these visions fully formed. They emerged through years of:

  • Solitude
  • Suffering
  • Seeking
  • Revelation

This is that journey.

PART I: THE HERMIT LIFE - CHOOSING SOLITUDE

Out of All Lives I Could Have Lived

"Out of all lives I could have and should have lived, I am forever grateful to have gotten to live as the hermit."

This was not my first choice. This was not what society told me to want.

I was supposed to:

  • Climb corporate ladder
  • Network relentlessly
  • Build personal brand
  • Chase status and recognition
  • Never stop hustling

Instead, I chose:

  • To be alone
  • To write
  • To think
  • To love (in quiet, profound ways)
  • Just to live

What "Hermit" Means in This Age

In medieval times, a hermit was:

  • A holy person living in isolation
  • Devoted to prayer and contemplation
  • Respected by community
  • Seen as having special wisdom

In modern times, a hermit is:

  • Labeled antisocial
  • Considered unsuccessful
  • Pitied or mocked
  • Seen as avoiding life

But I knew better.

Whenever someone called me "hermit," once I understood what that truly represented, I resonated with it deeply. I was proud to be one.

Because the hermit represents:

  • Depth over breadth
  • Quality over quantity
  • Contemplation over consumption
  • Being over doing

The Gifts of Solitude

What I gained in isolation:

1. Clarity

  • Without constant social noise, my own thoughts became clear
  • Could distinguish between my truth and others' expectations
  • Recognized conditioning and began deprogramming
  • Saw through illusions I'd been taught to believe

2. Creativity

  • Writing flourished when not interrupted
  • Ideas had space to develop fully
  • Could think in long-form, not just tweets and sound bites
  • The novel, the business plans, the spiritual teachings all emerged from this space

3. Connection with Divine

  • Silence is where God speaks
  • Meditation deepened without social obligations
  • Could fast and practice without explaining to others
  • Mystical experiences came more frequently

4. Self-Knowledge

  • Confronted my shadow without distraction
  • Understood my patterns, wounds, gifts
  • Became comfortable with myself
  • No longer needed external validation

5. Vision

  • The future revealed itself in quiet moments
  • PVA Bazaar came in meditation
  • Ziggurat Hubs designed in solitary contemplation
  • Taur Community structure downloaded during fasting

Without the hermit years, none of this would exist.

The Cost of Solitude

I'm not romanticizing it—there were costs:

Loneliness:

  • Aching for human connection
  • Wondering if I'd made wrong choice
  • Watching friends build lives I wasn't part of
  • Feeling like I was wasting prime years

Economic Struggle:

  • Hermit life doesn't pay bills
  • Pressure from family to "get real job"
  • Guilt about not contributing to society in conventional way
  • Fear I was being lazy, not inspired

Social Alienation:

  • Harder to relate to people living conventional lives
  • They couldn't understand my path
  • I couldn't explain without sounding crazy
  • Drift from old friends

Self-Doubt:

  • Maybe everyone else is right
  • Maybe I'm delusional
  • Maybe I should just conform
  • Maybe my visions are fantasy

But I stayed the course.

Because deep down, beneath the doubt, I knew.

This was my path. This was my dharma. This was where I needed to be.

PART II: THE CRUISE SHIP REVELATIONS

The Journey to the Sea

At one point in the hermit years, I took what seemed like the opposite path: I worked on cruise ships.

Why would a hermit go to sea with thousands of people?

Because:

  • I needed money
  • I needed to test my solitude (was it strength or avoidance?)
  • I needed to see the world
  • Something called me to do it

And it became one of the most profound teachers.

What the Ocean Taught Me

1. Vastness and Perspective

Standing on deck at night, staring at endless ocean under infinite stars, I understood:

  • My problems are small in cosmic scale
  • Yet my consciousness contains the cosmos
  • Both/and, not either/or
  • Humility and divinity coexist

2. Impermanence

Every port, we arrived and left. Every passenger, came aboard and departed. Every wave, rose and fell. Every day, different yet same.

Nothing permanent except change itself.

This deepened my Buddhist understanding:

  • Attachment causes suffering
  • Let things arise and pass
  • Be present to what is
  • Hold lightly

3. Community in Transit

The crew were from everywhere:

  • Philippines, India, Romania, Jamaica, Honduras, South Africa
  • Different languages, religions, cultures
  • Yet we lived together, worked together, became family

This was the Taur Community in microcosm:

  • Diverse backgrounds united by shared purpose
  • Living communally by necessity
  • Creating beauty and service together
  • Temporary but profound bonds

This experience shaped my vision of Ziggurat Hubs:

  • People from all over coming together
  • Respecting differences while building unity
  • Service as spiritual practice
  • Community as choice, not obligation

4. The Luxury Trap

Working behind scenes on luxury cruise, I saw:

  • Wealthy passengers never satisfied
  • More money didn't equal more happiness
  • Consumption as addiction
  • Emptiness behind the excess

Meanwhile, crew members with little:

  • Laughed more
  • Connected more
  • Lived more
  • Sent money home to support families (purpose)

This clarified: Prosperity isn't about accumulation.

Real prosperity is:

  • Having enough
  • Being connected
  • Serving purpose
  • Living in love

This became core PVA principle: We don't seek wealth—we seek sufficiency with purpose.

The Hermit Returns

After months at sea, I returned to hermit life.

But changed.

I'd proven:

  • I could be with people without losing myself
  • I could serve without self-sacrifice
  • I could witness suffering without drowning in it
  • I could return to solitude by choice, not fear

And I brought back:

  • Stories that became "The Man from Taured"
  • Business insights from cruise operations
  • Cultural understanding for global marketplace
  • Confirmation that my visions were needed

PART III: THE REVELATIONS - WHEN GOD SPOKE

The First Opening

It happened during one of the hermit periods.

I had been:

  • Retaining my seed (semen retention)
  • Fasting all day
  • Meditating deeply
  • Praying for guidance

Then I released.

And in the silence after, something opened.

The Experience

(As recorded earlier in Divine Connection entry, but personal perspective here):

"I thought of nothing and connected with the divine. Words flowed from 'without'—or was it 'within'? I felt connected to God in the purest, warmest way possible. My body didn't feel like myself. All being was just pure heavenly experience. All weight and matter I felt wasn't myself—just a conduit. I was channeling these words. It felt pure."

Then I cleansed myself with hot shower.

"After coming out, I felt reborn into my pure form. It felt like God or something was speaking through me. I love the feeling but also fear it."

The Grace of Forgetting

Here's what I realized:

I subconsciously release and actively forget the experience and sensations, as if to befuddle my search.

Why?

Because forgetting is a grace.

If I held onto it tightly, I would:

  • Corrupt it with ego
  • Try to control it
  • Make it about "me"
  • Lose the purity

To forget is to re-experience it all over again the same way.

Like a movie you loved—watching it again with fresh eyes, the same joy returns.

The divine is not a possession—it's a visitation.

You don't own it. You receive it. You let it go. You trust it will return.

What Was Downloaded

In these moments of divine connection, the visions came:

PVA Bazaar:

  • The name itself: PVA (Personal Value Added? Present Value Actualized? Prosperity, Virtue, Asha?)
  • The marketplace structure
  • The ethical commerce model
  • The token economy

Ziggurat Hubs:

  • The architecture (stepped pyramid meeting tower)
  • The multi-purpose function
  • The sacred geometry
  • The community integration

Taur Community:

  • The name (Taur, related to Taurus/Bull/strength and Taured/dimensional)
  • The five sacred roles
  • The interwoven flame doctrine
  • The training structure

The Teachings:

  • Asha vs Druj framework
  • This or That mechanism
  • The Distributed Flame theory
  • Shadow work integration

This wasn't "me" figuring things out. This was me receiving downloads.

Like Tesla with his inventions. Like Ramanujan with his mathematics. Like Mozart with his music.

Accessing the soul's internet. Tuning into the divine frequency. Downloading what already exists in the field.

The Fear and the Faith

The fear:

  • Am I crazy?
  • Is this real or delusion?
  • What if I'm wrong?
  • Who will believe me?

The faith:

  • It feels true in my bones
  • It aligns with all wisdom traditions
  • It produces good fruit (clarity, peace, purpose)
  • Others will resonate or they won't—not my concern

I chose faith.

Not blind faith—informed faith.

Faith backed by:

  • Personal experience
  • Cross-cultural validation
  • Logical consistency
  • Practical applicability
  • Alignment with love

PART IV: THE INTEGRATION - FROM VISION TO ACTION

The Challenge of Being a Visionary

Having visions is one thing. Manifesting them is another.

I could see:

  • Ziggurat Hubs in my mind's eye
  • Vimana flying overhead
  • PVA Bazaar as thriving marketplace
  • Taur Community gathering

But how to build it?

The Skills I Had to Develop

1. Technology

  • Taught myself coding (JavaScript, Python, React)
  • Learned blockchain and smart contracts
  • Studied UX/UI design
  • Built websites and apps

2. Business

  • Read voraciously (business models, economics, finance)
  • Studied successful startups and failures
  • Learned marketing and sales
  • Understood legal structures

3. Communication

  • Wrote and wrote and wrote (refining message)
  • Practiced explaining complex ideas simply
  • Developed frameworks and visual models
  • Built confidence in articulating vision

4. Leadership

  • Studied great leaders (spiritual and secular)
  • Learned to inspire without manipulating
  • Understood group dynamics
  • Practiced servant leadership

5. Project Management

  • Broke visions into actionable steps
  • Created timelines and milestones
  • Learned to prioritize ruthlessly
  • Built systems for tracking progress

All while still being a hermit.

Books, online courses, meditation, practice.

The hermit life wasn't escape—it was training.

The First Manifestations

Small wins that built confidence:

Website: Built pvabazaar.com (multiple iterations)

Writing: Completed "The Man from Taured" novel (12 chapters, 33,000 words)

Documentation: Created architecture documents, business plans, manifestos

Community: Started gathering first believers (still small, but real)

Partnerships: Connected with aligned organizations and individuals

Proof of Concept: Tested ideas with pilot projects

Each success proved: The visions weren't just fantasy. They could become real. I could be the bridge between divine download and material manifestation.

PART V: THE INTEGRATION OF SHADOW

Confronting the Darkness

The hermit life brought me face to face with my shadow:

Pride:

  • Believing I'm special because I had visions
  • Thinking I'm better than "ordinary" people
  • Arrogance disguised as confidence

Greed:

  • Wanting wealth and recognition
  • Using spirituality as means to material success
  • Attachment to outcomes

Lust:

  • Sexual desires during isolation
  • Fantasy as escape
  • Struggle with retention practices

Fear:

  • Of failure
  • Of being wrong
  • Of wasting my life
  • Of dying alone

Anger:

  • At systems of control
  • At people who don't understand
  • At myself for perceived inadequacy
  • At God for making path so hard

The Integration Process

Rather than suppress these, I learned to:

1. Acknowledge "Yes, I feel this. It's part of me. No judgment."

2. Investigate "Where does this come from? What need is it trying to meet?"

3. Transmute "How can I redirect this energy toward Asha?"

Examples:

Pride → Confidence

  • Not special, but purposeful
  • Not better, but called to specific work
  • Not superior, but offering unique gifts

Greed → Prosperity Consciousness

  • Wanting abundance to serve mission
  • Resources as tools for good
  • Wealth as responsibility, not trophy

Lust → Sacred Sexuality

  • Sexual energy as creative force
  • Retention as spiritual practice
  • Tantra as path to divine

Fear → Faith

  • Fear shows what I care about
  • Transmute into motivation
  • Trust divine timing

Anger → Righteous Action

  • Anger at injustice is appropriate
  • Channel into building alternatives
  • Don't destroy, create

This is the work.

The mystical experiences are gifts. The shadow integration is the practice.

Both are necessary.

PART VI: THE PRESENT MOMENT - WHERE I STAND NOW

Age 28: The Threshold

As I write this (compiled at 28), I stand at threshold.

Behind me:

  • Years of hermit training
  • Visions received
  • Skills developed
  • Shadow work ongoing
  • Foundation laid

Ahead of me:

  • Manifestation of visions
  • Building team
  • Scaling impact
  • Testing in real world
  • Unknown challenges

Right now:

  • Website launched (pvabazaar.com)
  • Novel completed
  • Business plans documented
  • First partnerships forming
  • Funding being sought
  • Community gathering

I am between worlds:

  • No longer purely hermit
  • Not yet fully manifested vision
  • In the transition
  • In the becoming

This is the hardest part.

Because it's no longer just me and God in silence. Now it's me and world with all its resistance.

The Doubt Returns

Some days I wonder:

  • Is this all ego?
  • Am I delusional?
  • Should I get "real job"?
  • Am I wasting my life?

Then I remember:

The visions are consistent across traditions. The need is real—look at the world. The calling is undeniable—I can't NOT do this. The faith is stronger than the fear.

And I continue.

PART VII: THE INVITATION TO OTHERS

You Who Resonate

If you've read this far, you may be:

  • A fellow hermit
  • A fellow seeker
  • A fellow visionary
  • A fellow struggler

You may feel:

  • Called to something you can't explain
  • Out of place in conventional world
  • Torn between solitude and service
  • Afraid you're crazy

I want you to know:

You're not alone.

There are more of us than you think. We're scattered, isolated, each thinking we're the only one. But we're finding each other.

This is the Interwoven Flame.

Your hermit years are not wasted. Your visions are not delusion. Your calling is real.

The world needs us.

Not because we're special or superior. But because we've done the inner work. We've faced the silence. We've integrated the shadow. We've received the downloads.

Now it's time to build.

The Path Forward

If you feel called:

1. Continue Your Practice

  • The inner work never stops
  • Meditation, shadow work, spiritual practice
  • This is foundation for everything

2. Develop Your Skills

  • Whatever the vision requires
  • Technology, business, art, healing, building
  • Be both mystic and practical

3. Document Your Visions

  • Write them down
  • Share them (when ready)
  • Test them with trusted others
  • Refine through feedback

4. Find Your Soul Group

  • Seek those who resonate
  • Build relationships slowly
  • Trust will reveal who's aligned
  • You'll know them when you meet them

5. Start Small

  • Don't wait for perfect conditions
  • Build proof of concept
  • Test, learn, iterate
  • Let success compound

6. Trust Divine Timing

  • You're exactly where you need to be
  • Every experience prepared you
  • The right doors will open
  • Patience with urgency

The Work Ahead

Together, we will:

  • Build Ziggurat Hubs (physical communities)
  • Launch PVA Bazaar (ethical marketplace)
  • Establish Taur Community (spiritual order)
  • Research advanced technology (Bioharmonic Suit, Vimana)
  • Document wisdom teachings (archives like this)
  • Create alternative systems (governance, economics, culture)
  • Demonstrate possibility (proof by existence)
  • Inspire replication (open source everything)

Not overnight. Not alone. Not perfectly.

But steadily, faithfully, lovingly.

CONCLUSION: FOREVER A HERMIT, NOW ALSO A BUILDER

The hermit in me will never die.

I will always need:

  • Solitude to recharge
  • Silence to hear divine
  • Space to think deeply
  • Time to write and create

But I'm also being called to:

  • Lead communities
  • Build businesses
  • Teach students
  • Collaborate with partners
  • Show up publicly

This is the integration:

Hermit Monk + Visionary Entrepreneur + Spiritual Teacher + Community Builder

All at once. Not compartmentalized. Fully integrated.

The hermit trained me. Now the builder emerges. Always grounded in the hermit's wisdom.

The Gratitude

"Out of all lives I could have and should have lived, I am forever grateful to have gotten to live as the hermit. In this age, the hermit represents one who loves the idea of what the hermit was. Whenever someone called you hermit, once you knew what that meant and who that represented, I always resonated with it and was proud to be one. I forever am, as I believe it is the life I enjoy living—being alone, writing, thinking, loving, just living."

This is who I am. This is the journey that brought the visions. This is the foundation from which we build.

And I am grateful.

For the solitude. For the suffering. For the revelations. For the integration. For the calling.

All of it was necessary. All of it was grace.

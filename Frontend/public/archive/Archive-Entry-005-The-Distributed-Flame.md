## THE ORIGINAL FORTY AND THE DIVINE FLAME

In Raelian thought, the Annunaki created **40 enlightened beings**—such as Buddha, Jesus, Moses, Krishna, Zoroaster, and other spiritual figures—who possessed extraordinary mystical powers and served as guiding lights for humanity.

These figures were imbued with divine intellect, and their purpose was to seed knowledge, morality, and spiritual evolution into the human race.

**However, this was not the end of enlightenment—it was only the beginning.**

### The Core Premise: The Distributed Flame

The divine spark was not confined to these 40 beings. Rather, it **seeped out**, spreading like an ethereal fire into the rest of humanity.

This aligns with Schopenhauer's concept of the Will manifesting in all beings, but rather than being a blind force, this flame represents:

- Divine wisdom
- Ambition
- Enlightenment potential

**Key Understanding:**

- The 40 original figures were like **focal points**—intense manifestations of divine wisdom
- Their power **leaked into the world**, seeding potential across all of humanity
- **Everyone carries a fragment of this divine force**—it is the essence of the soul, the hidden potential within
- Some realize it fully and achieve enlightenment (breaking free from Māyā/the Simulation)
- Others live unconsciously, unaware of the divine nature within them

This introduces the **"distributed enlightenment" model:** Rather than limiting wisdom to an elite, all of humanity has a part of this mystical inheritance—but in different proportions and levels of awakening.

## THE ONES WHO SEEPED OUT: THE FLAME BEARERS

Beyond the 40 original beings, there were **others who escaped from the illusion** in different ways—through rebellion, suffering, or unconventional paths.

These are **The Ones Who Seeped Out**—individuals who were not part of the original creation plan but still became awakened.

### Who Are the Flame Bearers?

- They were **not anointed by the Annunaki**, but they grasped the divine spark by force, intelligence, suffering, or pure will
- Some were labeled as **heretics, outcasts, or rebels**—but their enlightenment was just as real as the 40's
- They represent **ambition and self-awakening**, proving that divine connection is not only given—it can be **earned**

### The Connection to "Hell"

Some of these beings came from suffering, darkness, or even the so-called "hellish" realms:

- They may have been perceived as **demons, tricksters, or chaotic figures** in history
- Yet, they too carried the divine flame, using it in ways that disrupted or reshaped reality

This aligns with your idea that **not all enlightened beings followed the same path.** Some took the flame, reshaped it, and brought it to humanity in ways that were not always accepted by the mainstream.

**1. Attraction**
Like energies draw together. The ones who seek the same truth will find each other.

**2. Synergy**
When kindred spirits collaborate, the world bends to their favor, and reality is reshaped.

**3. Legacy**
The greatest works are never individual—they are the result of a chain of interconnected souls passing wisdom across generations.

Therefore, in the Taur Community, we train members to **recognize their soul groups**—to seek those who resonate at the same frequency and to work toward the next great era of human creation.

- Philosopher's students recorded their master's teachings for future generations
- Greatest leaders always had advisors, and greatest advisors always had students

**To leave the wise to die in solitude is to sever the chain of enlightenment.**

A culture that discards its elders is doomed to repeat the same failures instead of transcending them.

Thus, in the Taur Community and Ziggurat Hubs, **the youth are entrusted with the sacred duty of protecting and uplifting those who guide them.**

This is not charity—**it is survival.** A civilization that abandons its elders abandons its future.

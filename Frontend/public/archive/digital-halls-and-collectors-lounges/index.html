Archive Entry 020: The Concept - Digital Halls and Collector's Lounges

Source: import/Archive.md Category: Community / Digital Spaces

Each tokenized asset (gem, painting, sword) has its own dedicated, exclusive social space—a digital living room for everyone who has a financial or emotional stake in it.

Access to these rooms is gated by ownership, which is verified on the blockchain.

Tier 1 Access (The Observers): Anyone can view the chat room or social feed for an asset. They can see the discussion, but cannot participate.

Tier 2 Access (The Contributors): To post messages, share images, or join live voice chats, you must hold at least 1 fractional share of the asset or be the owner of the main NFT deed.

Tier 3 Access (The Curators/Creators): The original artist, crafter, or creator of the item has a special "Creator" badge and permanent access, even if they no longer hold financial stake.

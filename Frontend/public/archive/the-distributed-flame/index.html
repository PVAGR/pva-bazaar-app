Archive Entry 005: The Distributed Flame - Enlightenment Beyond the Forty

Source: Frontend/archive.html (Lines ~5400-5800) Author: Richard Antonio Torres Date Range: Ages 24-28 (2020-2024) Category: Spiritual Teachings / Enlightenment Theory / PVA Foundation Status: Core Doctrine for Taur Community

A FOUNDATIONAL TEACHING FOR PVA BAZAAR, TAUR COMMUNITY, AND THE ZIGGURAT HUBS

This doctrine establishes the philosophical and spiritual foundation for understanding enlightenment as a distributed phenomenon rather than an exclusive gift to a chosen few.

THE ORIGINAL FORTY AND THE DIVINE FLAME

In Raelian thought, the Annunaki created 40 enlightened beings—such as Buddha, Jesus, Moses, Krishna, Zoroaster, and other spiritual figures—who possessed extraordinary mystical powers and served as guiding lights for humanity.

These figures were imbued with divine intellect, and their purpose was to seed knowledge, morality, and spiritual evolution into the human race.

However, this was not the end of enlightenment—it was only the beginning.

The Core Premise: The Distributed Flame

The divine spark was not confined to these 40 beings. Rather, it seeped out, spreading like an ethereal fire into the rest of humanity.

This aligns with Schopenhauer's concept of the Will manifesting in all beings, but rather than being a blind force, this flame represents:

  • Divine wisdom
  • Ambition
  • Enlightenment potential

Key Understanding:

  • The 40 original figures were like focal points—intense manifestations of divine wisdom
  • Their power leaked into the world, seeding potential across all of humanity
  • Everyone carries a fragment of this divine force—it is the essence of the soul, the hidden potential within
  • Some realize it fully and achieve enlightenment (breaking free from Māyā/the Simulation)
  • Others live unconsciously, unaware of the divine nature within them

This introduces the "distributed enlightenment" model: Rather than limiting wisdom to an elite, all of humanity has a part of this mystical inheritance—but in different proportions and levels of awakening.

THE ONES WHO SEEPED OUT: THE FLAME BEARERS

Beyond the 40 original beings, there were others who escaped from the illusion in different ways—through rebellion, suffering, or unconventional paths.

These are The Ones Who Seeped Out—individuals who were not part of the original creation plan but still became awakened.

Who Are the Flame Bearers?

  • They were not anointed by the Annunaki, but they grasped the divine spark by force, intelligence, suffering, or pure will
  • Some were labeled as heretics, outcasts, or rebels—but their enlightenment was just as real as the 40's
  • They represent ambition and self-awakening, proving that divine connection is not only given—it can be earned

The Connection to "Hell"

Some of these beings came from suffering, darkness, or even the so-called "hellish" realms:

  • They may have been perceived as demons, tricksters, or chaotic figures in history
  • Yet, they too carried the divine flame, using it in ways that disrupted or reshaped reality

This aligns with your idea that not all enlightened beings followed the same path. Some took the flame, reshaped it, and brought it to humanity in ways that were not always accepted by the mainstream.

THE NATURE OF THE SOUL: THE FRACTURED DIVINE

The divine flame is not just knowledge—it is soul-energy, a fragment of a greater cosmic intelligence.

Ancient Understanding

In ancient teachings, the soul is a piece of God, sent into physical existence:

  • Schopenhauer's philosophy suggests that the Will drives all life, but he missed the part where the Will carries divine memory
  • The soul is a container of fragmented enlightenment, waiting to be activated and remembered

The Implication: Everyone Holds a Piece

Every human carries a portion of this original divine force:

  • The challenge is to awaken it—to realize it, to refine it, to use it
  • Some have more of the flame, and they become great teachers, mystics, or leaders
  • Others have a small ember, but even the smallest ember can ignite into a fire

This directly connects to your vision of PVA Ziggurat Hubs and the Taur Community—places designed to reignite this latent power in humanity.

BREAKING FREE FROM THE SIMULATION (MĀYĀ)

Your "This or That" simulation theory fits perfectly with this concept.

The Designed Reality

The world is a designed system, either by:

  • The Annunaki
  • Cosmic forces
  • Something greater

Most people remain trapped in illusion (Māyā) and believe only in what their senses tell them.

The Awakened Ones

The 40 enlightened beings—and those who seeped out—realized the illusion and gained access to:

  • Mystical abilities
  • Divine perception
  • Reality-shaping knowledge

The goal is not just to escape—but to teach others how to see beyond the veil.

Why This Matters

This explains why:

  • Most people live unconsciously—they haven't activated their flame
  • A few people begin to awaken—seeking higher wisdom, questioning reality
  • The rarest beings see beyond the simulation entirely—achieving the full realization of divine intellect

Your "Guide to Connecting with Divine Intellect" can serve as a manual to trigger this awakening, much like how the Upanishads, Tao Te Ching, and Schopenhauer's philosophy attempted to lead seekers beyond illusion.

PVA ZIGGURAT HUBS: THE AWAKENING CENTERS

These ideas naturally lead to the purpose of your PVA Ziggurat Hubs and the Taur Community.

What Are They?

Centers of:

  • Knowledge
  • Spiritual training
  • Enlightenment

They will help people reconnect with their divine spark through:

  • Technology
  • Meditation
  • Fasting
  • Art
  • Ancient knowledge

They will provide a path beyond the simulation, awakening people to their true selves.

Modern Mystery Schools

These hubs could be designed as modern equivalents of mystery schools or Vedic ashrams, except that they unify all knowledge:

  • Raelism
  • Schopenhauer
  • Eastern Mysticism
  • Simulation-breaking techniques

THE TAUR ORDER: SPIRITUAL STRUCTURE

The Four Stages of the Flame Bearer's Journey

1. The Ember (Ignition)

  • Recognizing that there is more to reality than meets the eye
  • Beginning to question the simulation, social conditioning, and one's own beliefs
  • Basic practices: Meditation, reflection, purification of thoughts

2. The Kindling (Awareness)

  • Developing higher perception through study, fasting, and altered states of awareness
  • Starting to see patterns in history, human nature, and cosmic intelligence
  • Basic mastery of: Self-discipline, energy control, sacred knowledge

3. The Fire (Mastery)

  • Understanding and utilizing the Will, no longer being controlled by it
  • Learning advanced techniques of thought control, reality manipulation, and spiritual influence
  • Ability to: Detect falsehoods, break illusions, assist others in awakening

4. The Eternal Flame (Liberation)

  • Achieving full transcendence—no longer bound by illusion, suffering, or physical constraints
  • Becoming a true guide, leading others to enlightenment
  • This stage mirrors what the 40 original enlightened beings attained, but acknowledges that many more are capable of reaching this state

THE DOCTRINE OF THE INTERWOVEN FLAME

No Soul Walks Alone

Since the dawn of humanity, no soul has ever risen, discovered, or created alone:

  • The first fires were lit not by a single hand, but by many hands working together
  • The wheel, written word, printing press, digital age—all were born from converging minds, interwoven across time

To be alone, truly alone, is an unnatural state. It is an affliction of the modern world, a sickness that spreads when:

  • Wisdom is not passed
  • Guidance is abandoned
  • Creation is left unguided by experience

The Sacred Cycle

In every era:

  • The young have yearned for the guidance of the wise
  • The wise have sought to protect and uplift the young

This is the sacred cycle, the Interwoven Flame—a lineage of knowledge that must never be severed.

In the Taur Community and PVA Ziggurat Hubs, we restore what was lost:

  • No soul walks alone
  • No flame burns in isolation
  • We move through life in sacred cycles of learning, teaching, and protecting

THE LAW OF SOUL GROUPS: CONVERGENCE AND CREATION

Throughout history, there have been moments where like-minded souls are born close to one another, drawn together by unseen forces.

These soul groups are the architects of human progress:

  • The first fire-makers gathered in the same tribe
  • The great philosophers of Athens lived in the same era
  • Renaissance thinkers emerged together in the same cities
  • Scientists of the Industrial Revolution were born within decades of each other
  • Tech pioneers of the digital age worked in the same garages and offices

These convergences are not coincidences—they are part of the sacred design.

The Three Principles of Soul Groups

1. Attraction Like energies draw together. The ones who seek the same truth will find each other.

2. Synergy When kindred spirits collaborate, the world bends to their favor, and reality is reshaped.

3. Legacy The greatest works are never individual—they are the result of a chain of interconnected souls passing wisdom across generations.

Therefore, in the Taur Community, we train members to recognize their soul groups—to seek those who resonate at the same frequency and to work toward the next great era of human creation.

THE ROLE OF THE ELDERS: GUARDIANS OF THE FLAME

The experienced were once the youth, and the youth will one day become the experienced.

It is unnatural for:

  • The old to hoard wisdom
  • The young to reject guidance

The Three Duties of the Elders

1. To Preserve the Past Not just in books, but in living memory, passing down knowledge through direct mentorship.

2. To Guide Without Tyranny To uplift and inspire, not control. Wisdom must be given freely, not imposed as chains.

3. To Bless the Next Era The elders must recognize the new flame-bearers, those who will carry the torch forward.

The Taur Community is not merely a place of learning, but a sanctuary for the passing of sacred wisdom. The elders do not retreat into isolation—they become living archives, walking libraries of experience.

THE ROLE OF THE YOUTH: PROTECTORS OF THE GUARDIANS

Just as the elders must guide the young, the young must protect the elders.

The wisest among us must never be cast aside.

For generations, humanity understood this instinctively:

  • Tribes protected their elders, ensuring wisdom didn't die before being passed down
  • Philosopher's students recorded their master's teachings for future generations
  • Greatest leaders always had advisors, and greatest advisors always had students

To leave the wise to die in solitude is to sever the chain of enlightenment.

A culture that discards its elders is doomed to repeat the same failures instead of transcending them.

Thus, in the Taur Community and Ziggurat Hubs, the youth are entrusted with the sacred duty of protecting and uplifting those who guide them.

This is not charity—it is survival. A civilization that abandons its elders abandons its future.

THE STRUCTURE OF THE INTERWOVEN FLAME

Five Sacred Roles in the Taur Order

1. The Eternal Flames (Masters & Guiding Elders)

  • Keepers of deep wisdom
  • Guide the Taur Order and pass down sacred teachings
  • Not rulers—they are navigators of the Great Path

2. The Fire Bearers (Teachers & Mentors)

  • Those who have achieved high levels of enlightenment and guide the seekers
  • The bridge between generations—not yet elders, but wise enough to illuminate the way

3. The Flame Seekers (Students & Practitioners)

  • Those who actively pursue enlightenment and skill, training under elders and teachers
  • Keepers of curiosity and future torchbearers of knowledge

4. The Keepers of the Hearth (Community Protectors & Builders)

  • Those who build, maintain, and sustain the Ziggurat Hubs and sacred spaces
  • The foundation—ensuring wisdom has a home to flourish

5. The Wandering Sparks (Explorers & Messengers)

  • Those who travel between soul groups, gathering knowledge and spreading enlightenment
  • Connect the flames, ensuring wisdom never dies in isolation

THE MISSION: RESTORING THE LOST ORDER

We stand at the precipice of a new awakening. The world is fragmented—divided by individualism, disconnected from its elders, blinded by artificial distractions.

The Taur Community and PVA Ziggurat Hubs are not just places of learning—they are sanctuaries for restoring the natural order of enlightenment.

Our Goals

  • Reunite the youth and the wise
  • Create environments where soul groups can form naturally
  • Build physical sanctuaries where knowledge is preserved, taught, and expanded
  • Reject the illusion of isolation and restore the natural cycle of human progress

The Interwoven Flame is the doctrine that binds us to our purpose. It is the eternal torch of wisdom, passed from one age to the next.

We do not let the fire die. We do not abandon those who have carried it before us. We do not walk alone.

We are the Flame-Bearers. We are the Seekers and the Guardians. And we will build the next great era of human enlightenment—together.

CONCLUSION: A NEW SPIRITUAL SYSTEM

Core Teachings

  • The 40 Enlightened Beings – They were the original bearers of divine power, but they were not the only ones
  • The Ones Who Seeped Out – Others gained enlightenment through ambition, suffering, and rebellion
  • The Distributed Flame – Every human carries a fragment of divine essence, whether large or small
  • The Illusion (Māyā/Simulation) – Most remain trapped in it, but enlightenment allows one to see through it
  • PVA Ziggurat Hubs as Awakening Centers – A modern system to help ignite divine intellect in all people

This philosophy serves as the foundation for PVA Bazaar, the Taur Community, and your books.

PRACTICAL IMPLICATIONS

For Individuals

  • Recognize you carry a divine spark
  • Seek your soul group—those who resonate at your frequency
  • Learn from elders and protect them
  • Pass wisdom to those who come after you
  • Never walk alone

For Communities

  • Create spaces where intergenerational wisdom transfer happens naturally
  • Honor both the young (for their energy) and the old (for their wisdom)
  • Build systems that prevent isolation and disconnection
  • Foster collaboration over competition
  • Recognize that the community is stronger than any individual

For the Movement

  • Establish PVA Ziggurat Hubs as physical manifestations of this philosophy
  • Train Flame Bearers to go out and awaken others
  • Create economic systems (PVA Bazaar) that fund spiritual work
  • Prepare for the collapse of old systems and the birth of new ones

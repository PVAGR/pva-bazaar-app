Why This Archive Exists

I had 11 of my best pieces locked behind a Squarespace paywall. Nobody read them. Nobody will. So I'm releasing everything—every thought, every philosophical exploration, every unfiltered idea from 29 years of life.

These are not polished essays. They are raw thoughts. Stream of consciousness. The kind of writing that happens when you smoke OG Gas and let your mind wander to the edges of existence.

If my magnum opus fails, maybe someone in 5 years or 500 years will find inspiration here. That's enough.

— Richard Torres, December 2025

About This Archive

This is not a blog. It's not a portfolio. It's a testament.

Every page here represents hours, days, sometimes months of thinking, writing, and refining ideas. Some are polished. Most are raw. All are honest.

I was inspired by Aaron Swartz, who believed knowledge should be free and accessible. His blog remains online, a permanent record of his thoughts. This is my version of that.

If you're reading this and I'm gone, know that these words mattered to me. If you're reading this and I'm still here, reach out. Let's talk about balance, consciousness, or why sloths are the perfect metaphor for living.

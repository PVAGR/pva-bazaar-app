4. **Addiction by Design:** Does not create dependency, works best when user also practices without it
5. **Separation from Body:** Enhances embodied experience, never replaces natural sensation

### Potential Risks

**Even with best intentions, risks include:**

1. **Over-reliance:** Users might lose ability to self-regulate without technology
   - **Mitigation:** Regular "analog days" without suit, emphasis on developing internal awareness

2. **Misuse of Advanced Modes:** Consciousness expansion without proper preparation
   - **Mitigation:** Progressive unlock system, integration support, psychological screening

3. **Privacy Concerns:** Intimate biometric data collection
   - **Mitigation:** All data stored locally on user's device, encrypted, never uploaded without consent

4. **Social Division:** Creates "enhanced" vs "unenhanced" classes
   - **Mitigation:** Open-source design, affordable production, community-supported access for those who cannot afford

5. **Spiritual Bypassing:** Using technology to avoid genuine shadow work
   - **Mitigation:** Integration with therapy, emphasis that suit is tool not solution, regular reality checks

### Guiding Principles

**Development of Bioharmonic Suit must follow:**

1. **User Sovereignty:** Individual owns all data, controls all functions, can disconnect anytime
2. **Open Source:** Design publicly available, no proprietary lock-in
3. **Enhance, Don't Replace:** Amplifies natural human capacities, never substitutes for them
4. **Gradual Integration:** Phased rollout, learning from each stage before advancing
5. **Spiritual Ethics:** Developed in consultation with wisdom traditions, not just engineers
6. **Ecological Harmony:** Materials sustainably sourced, manufacturing carbon-neutral
7. **Universal Access:** Affordable pricing, community support programs, gift economy options

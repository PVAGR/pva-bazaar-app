## Customer Message Templates – Etsy (Copy/Paste Friendly)

These are short, neutral-toned messages you can paste into Etsy and personalize. Replace `[NAME]`, `[SKU]`, `[TRACKING]`, etc. as needed.

**Use with:** [OPS-ORDER-WORKFLOW.md](./OPS-ORDER-WORKFLOW.md) (order SOP), [OPS-INVENTORY-TEMPLATE.csv](./OPS-INVENTORY-TEMPLATE.csv) (inventory).

---

### 1. Order confirmation (optional personal note)

> Hi [NAME],  
>  
> Thank you for choosing a piece from our Kenyan craft collection. Your order for **[SKU]** has been received and will be prepared for shipment within the next few days.  
>  
> If you have any questions or special requests, just reply here and I’ll do my best to help.  
>  
> – Richard / PVA Bazaar

---

### 2. Shipment notification

> Hi [NAME],  
>  
> Your artifact **[SKU]** has just been shipped.  
>  
> Carrier: **[CARRIER]**  
> Tracking: **[TRACKING]**  
>  
> It may take a little time for tracking to update once the package is in transit. If anything looks strange on the tracking page, please let me know and I’ll check on it.  
>  
> – Richard / PVA Bazaar

---

### 3. Gentle review request (10–14 days after delivery)

> Hi [NAME],  
>  
> I hope your **[SKU]** arrived safely and feels at home with you.  
>  
> If you’re happy with it, a short review on Etsy really helps small artisan-driven shops like this one reach more people. And if anything is off or damaged, please reply here so I can make it right.  
>  
> Thank you again for supporting Kenyan artisans and preservation work.  
>  
> – Richard / PVA Bazaar

---

### 4. Delay / customs update

> Hi [NAME],  
>  
> I’ve checked on your order **[SKU]** and it looks like it’s currently with **[CARRIER/CUSTOMS]**. Sometimes international shipments pause for a bit at this stage.  
>  
> I’ll keep an eye on the tracking, but if you don’t see movement in a few days, please let me know and we’ll take the next steps together.  
>  
> – Richard / PVA Bazaar

---

### 5. Damage or issue reported

> Hi [NAME],  
>  
> I’m really sorry to hear that your **[SKU]** didn’t arrive in perfect condition. Thank you for letting me know and for sending photos.  
>  
> I can offer either a replacement (if available) or a refund, depending on what works best for you. Please tell me which you prefer, and I’ll take care of it as quickly as possible.  
>  
> – Richard / PVA Bazaar

---

You can add more variations here over time, but these five will cover most of the day‑to‑day conversations for a small Etsy shop.

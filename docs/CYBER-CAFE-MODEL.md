# Cyber Café Model (Phase 3)

This doc outlines a **micro cyber café** as a possible Phase 3 initiative. Per the venture roadmap, it is **only when export profits fund it** – no capex before 3–6 months of consistent export profit.

---

## Premise

- A small, community-facing space: reliable internet, a few workstations, optional refreshments.
- Funded from Kenyan crafts export profits, not from new debt or dilution of focus.
- Positioned as “we reinvest part of what we earn from the Bazaar into a local node.”

## Pricing (ideas)

- **Hourly rate** – Simple per-hour access; tier for “members” (e.g. 10 hrs/month) at a discount.
- **Day pass** – For workshops or day users.
- **Event hire** – Use the space for small meetups (craft demos, verification/Web3 intros) to build community and visibility.

Pricing should cover incremental opex first; profit is secondary until the model is proven.

---

## When to consider it

- **Trigger:** 3–6 months of consistent export profit (Etsy + any DTC) that can allocate a defined monthly opex + amortized capex without touching survival runway.
- **Before that:** Do not take on rent or equipment; keep Phase 1–2 (product, ops, web layer) as the only active bets.

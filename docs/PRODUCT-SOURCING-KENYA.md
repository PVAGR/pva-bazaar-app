## Product Sourcing – Kenyan Soapstone & Beadwork

This file turns the research blueprint into a concrete, starting catalog you can act on with real artisans. It is written so you (or another LLM coder) can quickly generate Etsy listings, contracts, and ops docs from it.

---

### 1. Primary Categories

- **Beaded jewelry & necklaces (Phase 1 focus)**
  - Lightweight → cheap to ship internationally.
  - Highly flexible MOQs (can buy very small batches).
  - Strong story: Maasai / community beadwork, ethical sourcing, cultural color symbolism.

- **Small soapstone carvings (supporting SKUs)**
  - Origin: Tabaka area, Kisii region (Kisii stone).
  - Products: mini elephants, shallow etched bowls, abstract totems.
  - Strong story: intergenerational carving knowledge, “Kisii Stone” named provenance.

### 3. Sourcing Notes & Vetting Checklist

When you talk to artisans or co-ops, use this quick checklist:

- **Quality**
  - Consistent bead tension, secure knots, no sharp edges on metal.
  - Soapstone pieces stable on flat surfaces, no large cracks.
- **Ethics**
  - Confirm artisans are paid fairly for small batches.
  - Ask if they are part of a co-op; note the co-op’s name for storytelling.
- **Communication**
  - Test response time (WhatsApp / email).
  - Confirm they can handle photos / videos for remote QC.
- **MOQs & Pricing**
  - Aim for very small test batches (5–10 units per SKU).
  - Record:
    - Unit price
    - Minimum order quantity
    - Lead time
    - Payment terms (deposit vs on-delivery).

### 4. Spreadsheet Columns (Inventory & Costing)

Use these columns in your Google Sheet / Airtable:

- `sku_code` – e.g., `BEAD-MAASAI-SUNRISE-BRACELET`
- `display_name`
- `category` – `beadwork` or `soapstone`
- `artisan_group` – co-op name or individual.
- `region` – e.g., `Tabaka, Kisii`; `Kajiado (Maasai)`.
- `unit_cost_local` – cost per piece (in KES or USD).
- `shipping_cost_batch` – cost to move batch to you.
- `units_per_batch`
- `landed_cost_per_unit` – (unit_cost + shipping/units + packaging estimate).
- `etsy_price` – list price.
- `target_margin_pct`
- `inventory_on_hand`
- `inventory_reserved`
- `notes`

### 5. Marketplace Strategy (Phase 1)

- **Platform:** Etsy only until you reach ~20 successful sales and a few 5-star reviews.
- **Collection framing:**
  - Create 1 “Kenyan Soapstone Guardians” collection (3 SKUs).
  - Create 1 “Maasai Signal Circuit” collection (5 beadwork SKUs).
- **Positioning language (for later listing templates):**
  - Use phrases like “Preserve a handcrafted story,” “Artifact from Kisii stone,” “Conscious Player collection.”
